# v17: the river's year (11 November 2026)

Answer to the owner's "elevate the existing game" brief. Direction and the mechanic's rules are in
`docs/DIRECTION.md`; this file records what was found, what changed and how it was checked.

## Audit (short form)

How it was done: the source was read, and the game was played in headless Chromium at 844×390 (a phone held
sideways) by a script that taps the real buttons and the map (`tools/scratch`, not committed). Days were
advanced through the debug stepper where waiting would have taken minutes. Nothing was heard: there is no
audio output in this environment.

Strengths kept: the hand-inked isometric island and its animals; the fixed-step, deterministic simulation with
golden fixtures; raids fought on the map with a forecast that uses the fight's own code; site-bound production
(soil, moisture, trees, rock, clean water); the 25 public-domain pieces with provenance.

Problems found, by player impact:

| # | Finding | Evidence | Fix in v17 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Nothing distinguishes the island over time: the land never changes, so placement is solved once | code: production reads only static terrain | the river's year |
| 2 | On a sideways phone the next objective was hidden; the chip showed only the chapter title | observed in screenshots | objective is the headline |
| 3 | The per-day change of each resource was hidden on a sideways phone | observed | restored under each number |
| 4 | The Build menu showed 27 cards in chapter 1, most unaffordable in kind | observed | buildings needing timber or stone appear once some has been gathered |
| 5 | The resource bar gave no way to the Daily ledger | observed | tapping the bar opens it |

## Changes

- `src/world/river.js` (new), with hooks in production, the step, raid planning and scheduling, the save
  schema (two optional fields), the renderer, the clock, the clock panel, the placement bar and the Army panel.
- Opening: the first message points at the river's low bank and its Day 5 flood.
- HUD and Build menu fixes listed above (`public/compact.css`, `public/extras.css`, `src/ui/home.js`).

## Verification

Automated: `npm test` 104 pass, 0 fail, 2 skipped (11 new in `tests/river.test.js`); `npm run typecheck` clean;
`tests/browser/smoke.mjs`, `raid.mjs`, `layout.mjs` pass (sideways phone keeps 78% of the map).

Seen in screenshots (headless Chromium, 844×390 at 2×): the opening message; the blue bank and the placement
note while placing a farm; the flood, low water and ice on the map with the clock words; the clock panel's
river section; at 1440×900, the flood on the map and the clock bar's line ("River in flood until Day 8").
Not looked at in the browser: the Army panel's flood and low-water sentences; the clock bar's tooltip that
lists the coming changes on a wide screen (the phone's clock panel has no wide-screen twin).

Measured: requestAnimationFrame intervals over 8 s, new campaign, 1×, headless Chromium with software
rendering on a 2-core cloud container (not a device, no GPU).

overlay. A dense late-game settlement was not measured in this round.

Not verified: sound and music (cannot be heard here); any real phone or tablet; screen readers; whether
players find the river interesting (no one has played v17).
