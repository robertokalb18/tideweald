TIDEWEALD — RENAISSANCE CONSORT · 29 September 2026

Twenty-five existing works, composed between c. 1470 and 1612. The compositions are in
the public domain. Published editions supply all melodic AND harmonic note events.
No AI compositions, newly composed chords, invented bass lines or sampled recordings.
Instrumental realizations of vocal and organ scores retain the written polyphony.
Tempo, instrument choice, balance and exact whole-score repeats are interpretive.

Ten Philippe de Monte editions: Aaron Dalton, 2014, CC BY 4.0.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Changes: instrumental timbres, tempo, velocity, whole-score repetition and synthesis.
The remaining fifteen editions are explicitly marked Public Domain by Mutopia.
Tallis's edition includes editorial ficta identified in the publisher's source.
These are present in its published MIDI, not newly composed here.

Fifteen pieces use the publisher's own LilyPond MIDI. For the ten pieces added on
29 September 2026 the publisher MIDI could not be downloaded, so their MIDI
(renaissance-sources/*.ly2midi.mid) was derived from the unmodified Mutopia LilyPond
source (taken from github.com/MutopiaProject/MutopiaProject, commit recorded in
renaissance-sources/manifest.json) with scripts/ly2midi.py. That interpreter
reproduces all fifteen publisher MIDI files note-for-note. As in LilyPond's MIDI,
voices sharing a staff are played by one instrument and unisons sound once.

Tideweald's synthetic audio/MIDI realizations are released under CC BY 4.0.
Credit: Historical composers; edition maintainers below; Tideweald instrumental
realization (2026). No sampled performance or commercial soundfont is used.
Original publisher MIDI, notation files, PDFs and metadata are supplied separately.
The soundtrack license does not change the game's code or artwork licensing.
